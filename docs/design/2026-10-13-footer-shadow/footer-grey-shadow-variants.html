Notes

Shadow reads as one light source from above: everything offsets downward, and a tight contact layer sits under a wide, low-alpha ambient layer. Two rules do most of the work — keep filter: drop-shadow() on .memo-w (a box-shadow would draw a rectangle and destroy the torn bottom edge), and never lift the paper twice.
